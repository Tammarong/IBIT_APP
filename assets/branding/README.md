# ITD visual identity

`itd-header.png` is the faculty's header wordmark from https://www.itd.kmutnb.ac.th/img/Logo/Logo-Header.png.

`itd-example.jpg` is the faculty emblem shown on its official logo-download page at https://www.itd.kmutnb.ac.th/e-service-3.php. The Android launcher uses the same image in `android/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/itd_emblem.jpg`, placed within a white square by Android drawable XML without altering the emblem artwork.

The app's navy `#192F59`, white and orange visual palette follows the faculty website's menu and room page. Mitr typefaces in `assets/fonts` come from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/mitr and are distributed under the accompanying `assets/fonts/OFL.txt` license.
